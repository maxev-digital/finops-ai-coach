# IRMAA Planning — Medicare Premium Surcharges

Source: CMS.gov, 42 U.S.C. §1395r, IRS Notice 2024-80.

---

## What Is IRMAA

Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount. A surcharge added to Medicare Part B (medical insurance) and Part D (prescription drugs) premiums for higher-income beneficiaries. IRMAA is determined annually based on your MAGI from 2 years prior.

- 2025 premiums: based on 2023 MAGI
- 2026 premiums: based on 2024 MAGI

This 2-year lookback is critical for planning — decisions made today affect premiums two years from now.

## Why IRMAA Matters More Than People Expect

**The cliff effect:** IRMAA is a step function, not a gradual scale. One dollar over a threshold can trigger hundreds of dollars per month in additional premiums.

**Example:** MFJ couple, MAGI of $211,500 in 2023. Standard Part B premium in 2025: $185/month × 2 = $370/month combined. If their MAGI had been $213,000 — $1,500 more — they'd jump to Tier 1: $259/month × 2 = $518/month. One year's extra premiums: $1,776 more. This is a marginal tax rate of 118% on that last $1,500 of income.

**Compound effect:** IRMAA tiers affect both spouses independently. A couple where each triggers IRMAA pays double. Over a 10-year retirement, paying Tier 1 instead of standard = $17,760 extra in Part B premiums alone.

## IRMAA and Roth Conversions

Roth conversions increase MAGI and can trigger IRMAA. This is the most common planning error: converting aggressively in years 63–70 without checking IRMAA impact on Medicare at 65–72.

**The lookback:** Conversions in 2025 (at age 63) affect 2027 premiums (when you're 65, just starting Medicare). Plan the conversion ceiling around the IRMAA thresholds, not just the income tax brackets.

**Practical ceiling for MFJ couple:** Roth conversion limited to keep total MAGI below $212,000 in years 2 years before Medicare eligibility (keep premiums at standard rate).

---

## IRMAA and RMDs

RMDs are ordinary income and increase MAGI. A large traditional IRA/401(k) balance can force IRMAA surcharges indefinitely through retirement. This is the "RMD tsunami" problem — your required distributions may push you into IRMAA tiers even if you don't need the income.

Roth conversions during the pre-RMD window specifically address this: reduce future RMDs → reduce future MAGI → reduce or eliminate future IRMAA surcharges.

## IRMAA Appeals — Life-Changing Events

You can appeal IRMAA if a "life-changing event" reduced your income since the base year. Qualifying events:

- Marriage, divorce, or death of spouse
- Work stoppage or reduction (retirement, layoff)
- Loss of income-producing property (foreclosure, disaster)
- Loss of pension income
- Employer settlement payment

File Form SSA-44 (Medicare Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount — Life-Changing Event). SSA will use a more recent tax year's income (or estimate) rather than the 2-year lookback. This is highly valuable when someone retires mid-year and their prior-year income doesn't reflect their new situation.

**Action point:** If you retired in 2024, file SSA-44 to avoid paying 2026 IRMAA based on your full 2024 working income. Use your estimated 2025 or 2026 income instead.

## Other Income That Affects MAGI for IRMAA

IRMAA MAGI = AGI + tax-exempt interest. Items that count:
- Wages, salary, self-employment income
- Taxable Social Security benefits
- Pension and annuity income
- Distributions from Traditional IRAs and 401(k)s (including RMDs)
- Roth conversions
- Capital gains (realized)
- Rental income
- Tax-exempt municipal bond interest (added back)

Items that do NOT count:
- Roth IRA qualified distributions
- Health Savings Account (HSA) withdrawals for qualified medical expenses
- Reverse mortgage proceeds
- Life insurance proceeds
- Return of basis (non-deductible IRA distributions)

## Strategies to Manage IRMAA

1. **Roth conversion ceiling:** Model the exact IRMAA threshold and stop conversions before crossing. Even converting $1 less may save thousands in premiums 2 years out.

2. **QCD instead of RMD withdrawal:** Satisfies RMD without increasing MAGI. If you plan to give to charity anyway, use QCDs at 70½ to reduce MAGI.

3. **Asset location for capital gains:** Keep high-turnover funds in IRA (capital gains not reported on 1040). Tax-efficient equity index funds in taxable (minimal distributions).

4. **Tax-gain harvesting below IRMAA threshold:** In low-income years, realize gains up to (but not crossing) the threshold.

5. **Municipal bonds:** Tax-exempt interest is added back for IRMAA. Munis reduce regular taxable income but do NOT help IRMAA. Less useful for this specific planning issue than commonly assumed.

6. **I-Bonds and Series EE Bonds:** Interest deferred until redemption. Can control the year you recognize interest. Don't redeem in high-income years.

## IRMAA Planning Timeline

| Age | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 60–62 | Model projected MAGI in years 63–70. Plan Roth conversion ceiling. |
| 63 | Begin large Roth conversions — Medicare starts at 65, lookback is 2 years |
| 64 | Last high-conversion year before Medicare. Watch 2025-base premiums. |
| 65 | Medicare begins. File SSA-44 if retired recently and income dropped. |
| 70½ | QCDs become available. Use for RMDs to manage MAGI. |
| 73/75 | RMDs begin. Review annual IRMAA exposure. Recalibrate conversion strategy. |
